UWChoose is a course planning platform for University of Waterloo Mathematics students to explore degree requirements and course paths.

- Designed prerequisite dependency graphs to model course relationships and support valid academic planning.
- Built interactive tools for course search, filtering, and long-term degree planning.
- Used AI-assisted development workflows to prototype features, debug backend logic, and improve development efficiency.
